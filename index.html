This is Buttermark's first alpha. It writes, reads and keeps your files safe; the edges are still rough and the installers aren't signed yet, so your computer will warn you once. Tell us what felt good and what didn't: feedback.

Windows

Windows protected your PC More info Run anyway

Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" screen the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. It won't ask again.

macOS

System Settings Privacy & Security Open Anyway

macOS will say Buttermark can't be opened. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Once is enough.

Linux: the .deb is for Ubuntu 24.04 or newer. The AppImage needs glibc 2.39; mark it executable, then run it.

The feel

Type two asterisks around a word and watch them dissolve when you leave. Arrow back in and they return. Headings, lists and code all behave this way, so the page looks like the page while you're still writing it.

Ask your agent

Select a sentence, press Ask, say what you want. Leave a few around the draft. One button hands them to whatever agent you already use, and the answers land back in your text with a highlighter wash and three small buttons: keep, take back, ask again. Buttermark never calls a model. Your agent, your account, your file.

Yours

Buttermark writes ordinary .md files to your own disk. No account, no vault to tend, nothing leaves your machine unless you send it. Like butter: you buy it, it's yours, spread it how you like.

Open any .md your tools left behind. Sticky table headers, footnotes, a reading ruler, a skim view. It reads as well as it writes.
